Bring the world of Hârn to your virtual tabletop with the HârnMaster 3.5E ruleset for Fantasy Grounds Unity. Developed with the kind permission of Columbia Games, Inc., this ruleset takes the heavy mathematical lifting out of HârnMaster’s legendary simulation, allowing you and your players to focus on the story and survival.

#Key Features
Automated Combat Resolution: The ruleset orchestrates the full three-step combat sequence: Attack, Defense, and Damage. The interactive Combat Resolution Window (CRW) automatically calculates the outcome between an attacker's strike and the defender's chosen response (Block, Counterstrike, Dodge, or Ignore) using official HârnMaster combat matrices, resolving tactical advantage and damage instantly.

Dynamic Injury & Penalty System: Wounds are tracked by strike location, aspect (Blunt, Edge, Point, Fire, Frost), and severity level (Minor, Serious, Grievous). Universal and Physical penalties calculate dynamically, automatically modifying skill rolls, combat actions, and trauma states as characters suffer wounds or heal.

Advanced Strike Locations & Armor Layering: Equipped armor automatically feeds its Blunt, Edge, Point, and Fire protection values directly into a dedicated 32-location strike table with high, mid, and low aim zones.

Streamlined Character Sheets: Core attributes, characteristics, and Skill Bases (SB) are calculated automatically, with full support for Sunsign modifiers and in-sheet skill development tracking (ML increases).

Print-Ready 8-Page Character Export: Export any character to automatically generate a beautifully styled, print-ready 8-page HTML character dossier matching the canonical HârnMaster 3.5E layout (for A4/Letter printing, PDF archiving, or offline reference).

Complete Supernatural Powers & Mystic System: Full support for Shek-Pvar arcane magic across all seven chantries (Gray and the six Convocations), divine clerical invocations, and psionics. Spells feature automated four-tier success resolution (CS, MS, MF, CF) with integrated saving throws and action automation.

Intelligent NPC & Bestiary Import: Import stat blocks directly from official HârnMaster publications. The ruleset automatically cleans up text formatting, organizes skills into standard tactical hierarchy (Initiative, Dodge, Unarmed, Combat, and Standard), and generates ready-to-use combat actions.

Automated Trauma & Shock Pipeline: Handles shock checks, recovery rolls, and transitions between consciousness, incapacitation, and medical shock states (Healing Rate H5) automatically.

Integrated GM Tools & Realism Toggles: Customized Combat Tracker displaying fatigue, strike locations, wounds, and status widgets at a glance. Optional GM realism settings allow toggling knockbacks, stumble rolls, fumbles, amputations, and bloodloss tracking.
