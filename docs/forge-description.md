The HârnMaster 3.5E ruleset implements Columbia Games' percentile RPG system for Fantasy Grounds Unity, providing full automation for combat, injuries, skills, and magic.

#Key Features
- **Combat Resolution**: Automated resolution of Attack, Defense (Block, Counterstrike, Dodge, Ignore), and Damage via the interactive Combat Resolution Window.
- **Hit Locations & Armor**: 32 body locations with High/Mid/Low aim zones and layered protection values (Blunt, Edge, Point, Fire).
- **Injury & Trauma System**: Wounds tracked by location and severity; automatic calculation of Universal Penalty (UP), Physical Penalty (PP), and Shock recovery rolls.
- **Character Management**: Automatic computation of Skill Bases (SB), Mastery Levels (ML), and Sunsign modifiers, with integrated skill development tracking.
- **Magic & Psionics**: Full support for Shek-Pvar magic (all 6 Convocations plus Gray), clerical invocations, and psionics with 4-tier success resolution (CS, MS, MF, CF).
- **NPC Import**: Text parser for official stat blocks with automatic OCR cleanup, skill ordering, and attack action generation.
- **Character Print Export**: Generates a print-ready 8-page HTML character dossier (A4/Letter) for tabletop play or offline reference.
- **Combat Tracker**: Displays fatigue, strike locations, active injuries, and status widgets at a glance.
- **Optional Rules**: GM toggles for knockbacks, stumble rolls, fumbles, amputations, and bloodloss.
